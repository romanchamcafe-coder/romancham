Made-to-order (coffee, juice, shakes)

No batch. When it sells, the app back-flushes — automatically subtracts raw ingredients (beans, milk) from Inventory. Skip Production/Transfer for these.

Made-to-stock (cakes, brownies, ice cream)

Follow the full line: Production → Store → Transfer → Display. When it sells, the app subtracts from Display, not from raw materials.

Kitchen in-charge — your daily routine

1Morning: produce
Open Production Log, record each batch you make. Set an expiry date.
2Fill the counter
Use Stock Transfer to move today's items Store → Display (repeat-yesterday is fastest).
3During service
Log any Wastage immediately with a reason. Never guess later.
4Closing
Do a Physical Count of Display, run Sync sales → display, check the Dashboard.
