☕ Made-to-order items (coffee, juice, shakes)

No batch. When it sells, the app back-flushes — it automatically subtracts the raw ingredients (beans, milk…) straight from Inventory. Skip Production/Transfer for these.

🍰 Made-to-stock items (cakes, brownies, ice cream)

Follow the full line: Production → Store → Transfer → Display. When it sells, the app subtracts from Display (the batch you moved to the counter), not from raw materials.

👨‍🍳 Kitchen in-charge — your daily routine

1Morning: produce
Open Production Log, record each batch you make (cakes, ice cream…). Set an expiry date.
2Fill the counter
Use Stock Transfer to move today's items Store → Display. Repeat-yesterday button is fastest.
3During service
Log any Wastage immediately with a reason. Never guess later.
4Closing
Do a Physical Count of Display, then open Sync sales → display and check the Dashboard.
